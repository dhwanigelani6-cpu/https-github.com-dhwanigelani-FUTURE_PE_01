# Business Research and Requirement Analysis

## 1. Project Background

Small local businesses need clear website content to explain their products, services and benefits to potential customers.

Writing suitable website copy can take time, especially when business owners are unsure how to present their services online.

Sarthana Cafe Copy Studio demonstrates a prompt-based approach to this problem.

## 2. Selected Business Category

Primary category: Cafe and Restaurant

Target location: Sarthana Jakatnaka, Surat, Gujarat, India

The project uses cafes and restaurants as its primary example. The content generator is also designed to support other local business categories.

## 3. Target Audience

- Cafe and restaurant owners
- Small business owners
- New local businesses
- Business owners creating their first website
- Businesses that need clear marketing copy

## 4. Common Content Requirements

A local business website may require:

- Homepage headline and introduction
- About Us section
- Product or service descriptions
- Business benefits
- Calls to action
- Contact and enquiry content
- SEO title and meta description

## 5. Identified Challenges

1. Business owners may struggle to write effective headlines.
2. Website content may be too generic.
3. Service descriptions may not explain customer benefits.
4. Calls to action may be unclear.
5. The writing style may not match the target audience.
6. Business claims may be included without verification.

## 6. Proposed Solution

The project uses a structured prompt workflow to analyze business details, draft website copy and improve the final content.

Users provide the business name, category, location, target audience, products or services, unique selling points, preferred tone and business goal.

The workflow uses these details to prepare relevant content sections.

## 7. Expected Benefits

- Faster content drafting
- Consistent brand tone
- Clearer website messaging
- Reusable prompt templates
- Easier content editing
- Better organization of marketing content

## 8. Assumptions and Limitations

The project assumes that users provide accurate business information.

No specific local competitor prices, customer ratings or business performance figures are claimed in this research.

The generated copy should be reviewed by the business owner before publication.

Live AI output depends on the availability and configuration of an AI service.

## 9. Conclusion

The research supports a structured content-generation workflow for local businesses. The proposed system connects business analysis, website copywriting and CTA improvement into a reusable process.
