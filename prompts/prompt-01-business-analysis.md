# Prompt 1: Local Business Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze the supplied business information before generating website content.

## Prompt Template

You are a local business research and website content strategist.

Analyze the following business details:

- Business name: [BUSINESS_NAME]
- Business category: [BUSINESS_TYPE]
- Location: [LOCATION]
- Target audience: [TARGET_AUDIENCE]
- Products or services: [PRODUCTS_SERVICES]
- Unique selling points: [USPS]
- Brand tone: [BRAND_TONE]
- Primary business goal: [BUSINESS_GOAL]

Complete the following tasks:

1. Describe the target audience.
2. Identify the likely needs of these customers.
3. Explain the benefits supported by the supplied business information.
4. Identify the main differentiator.
5. Recommend a suitable website messaging approach.
6. Identify the most suitable conversion goal.
7. List any missing details that require confirmation.

## Rules

- Use simple and clear language.
- Do not invent awards, ratings, testimonials or statistics.
- Do not assume that a business offers a service unless it is specified.
- Clearly identify missing information.
- Separate supplied facts from assumptions.

## Expected Output

A structured business analysis with headings for audience, customer needs, benefits, differentiators, website messaging, conversion goal and missing information.

## Prompt Logic

This prompt creates a clear understanding of the business before copywriting begins. Its output becomes the input for Prompt 2.
