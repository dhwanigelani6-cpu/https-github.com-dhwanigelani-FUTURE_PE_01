# Prompt 3: CTA Optimization and Quality Check

## Purpose

Improve the generated copy and prepare calls to action that match the business goal.

## Prompt Template

You are a website conversion copywriter and content quality reviewer.

Review the following website content:

[GENERATED_WEBSITE_COPY]

Business goal:
[BUSINESS_GOAL]

Target audience:
[TARGET_AUDIENCE]

Brand tone:
[BRAND_TONE]

Complete these tasks:

1. Create three different CTA variations.
2. Match each CTA to the selected business goal.
3. Improve unclear or repetitive sentences.
4. Check consistency of brand tone.
5. Correct grammar and spelling.
6. Check whether each statement is supported by the supplied facts.
7. Flag unsupported claims and missing business information.
8. Provide a suitable SEO title and meta description.

## CTA Examples

For visits:
- Plan Your Visit
- Explore Our Menu

For enquiries:
- Contact Our Team
- Send an Enquiry

For bookings:
- Request a Booking
- Check Availability

Use only the CTA options relevant to the business.

## Rules

- Do not invent customer reviews, ratings or guarantees.
- Do not add unverified offers or prices.
- Keep CTAs clear and action-oriented.
- Preserve the original business meaning.
- Clearly flag any details that require confirmation.

## Expected Output

Provide the revised website copy, three relevant CTA options, an SEO title, a meta description and a short quality-check report.

## Prompt Logic

This prompt improves clarity, checks factual consistency and adapts the final content to the business goal.
