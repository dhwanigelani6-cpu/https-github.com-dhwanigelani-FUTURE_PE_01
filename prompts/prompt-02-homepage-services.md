# Prompt 2: Homepage and Service Copy

## Purpose

Generate website-ready content using the business information and analysis from Prompt 1.

## Prompt Template

You are a professional website copywriter for local businesses.

Use the business details and analysis below to create clear, persuasive website content.

Business details:
[BUSINESS_DETAILS]

Business analysis:
[BUSINESS_ANALYSIS]

Generate the following sections:

1. Homepage headline
2. Homepage subheading
3. Short homepage introduction
4. About Us section
5. Products or services descriptions
6. Why Choose Us section
7. Three key customer benefits
8. A short closing paragraph

## Writing Requirements

- Match the selected brand tone.
- Use simple, natural language.
- Focus on customer needs and business benefits.
- Keep headlines concise and meaningful.
- Make each service description specific to the supplied information.
- Avoid repetitive phrases and exaggerated promises.
- Do not invent business facts.

## Expected Output

Organize the content under separate headings so it can be copied into individual website sections.

## Prompt Logic

This prompt converts the business analysis into usable website copy. It keeps the content connected to the business category, audience and supplied differentiators.
